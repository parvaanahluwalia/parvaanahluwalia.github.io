About Parvaan

I’m Parvaan, a Grade 9 student in the IB Middle Years Programme. I like problems where the answer has to physically work: a robot that has to find the ball, a car that has to pass the rules, a kart that has to carry a driver.

Most of my projects start in CAD (Fusion 360, SketchUp, or code-driven CAD with CadQuery) and end with something I can test. Lately I’ve been pulling AI into the loop too, running models locally on my own machine.

This site is my logbook. Every project has its own page: what I built, the decisions behind it, and what comes next.

that actually moves.
