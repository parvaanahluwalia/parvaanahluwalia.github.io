01 The brief

In 2023–24 I organised a robotics and design workshop for Grade 6 students at Pathways World School, and planned and ran it on my own.

02 What I did

  1. Independently planned and conducted the workshop at school.
  2. Introduced Grade 6 students to robotics, modelling and design.
  3. Encouraged them to explore these areas for themselves.
  4. After the workshop, students brought their own LEGO pieces and created their own projects.
